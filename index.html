Before you start

AETHER helps you track and understand your health indicators on a mission.

  • It is decision support, not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat.
  • In an emergency, follow your mission medical procedures and contact the flight surgeon immediately.
  • Reference ranges are generic. Your flight surgeon's limits take priority.
  • Your data stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded unless you export it.
  • Crew data shown at first launch is simulated demo data.
